# Building DSX AI Factory Projects Together at KubeCon North America 2026

On Monday, November 9, the day before KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026 opens in Salt Lake City, NVIDIA is hosting the DSX OS Contributor Workshop. For one afternoon, maintainers, product managers, partners, and contributors will work side by side on [DSX AI Factory](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory) projects in the Wasatch Ballroom at the Radisson Hotel Salt Lake City Downtown.

The workshop is built around one idea: the fastest way to grow an open source project is to put the people who use it in the same room with the people who build it. Instead of talks, the afternoon is about working on real issues together, getting pull requests reviewed live, and having direct conversations about where each project goes next.

## Why a contributor workshop

Bringing the DSX OS projects together in [github.com/dsx-ai-factory](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory) gave the projects one home for code. The next step is making it easy for people outside NVIDIA to take part in that code: to find a good first issue, get a development environment working, and see their first contribution merged.

That first contribution is often the hardest. Setting up a project, understanding its CI, and knowing who to ask can take days when you do it alone. With a maintainer at the same table, much of that hurdle goes away in minutes. An [earlier DSX OS Contributor Workshop](https://lnkd.in/p/g5Cd-yCM) showed this: some participants opened pull requests the same afternoon, others went deep on project roadmaps, and many said the personal connections they made were what would keep them contributing.

## How the afternoon works

Each participating project gathers its product and engineering leads alongside attendees. After a short orientation, attendees help shape the contribution time agenda for roadmap roundtables. PMs and engineering are excited to listen, share, and build together.

- **Contribution sprint:** claim an issue, open a draft pull request, run CI, and get a live review from a maintainer. Attendees are encouraged to bring their AI coding assistants.
- **Roadmap roundtables:** bring your organization's use cases and integration ideas to the product managers and maintainers who shape the roadmap.

The afternoon closes with a Show and Tell, where each project table shares what they worked on, followed by time for networking with Bob Wise and Warren Barkley and the first in-person community meetings, where project communities kick off their regular meetings.

The workshop ends with something new for DSX AI Factory: the first in-person community meetings. More details: [Launching Open Communities for DSX AI Factory Projects](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory/community/blob/main/articles/dsx-ai-factory-community-meetings.md).

## Beyond the workshop

A workshop is only a starting point. The goal is that contributions started in Salt Lake City keep moving afterward, through ongoing review, project Slack channels, and the new community meetings. Conversations started at the roundtables will feed into each project's roadmap.

If you cannot make it on Monday, there are other ways to connect:

- Request an invite to the DSX AI Factory Slack workspace at [inviter.co/dsx-ai-factory](https://inviter.co/dsx-ai-factory) and join the channel for the project you care about.
- Visit NVIDIA booth #170 from November 10 to 12 for short technical Q&A sessions with maintainers. See [Meet the NVIDIA Experts at KubeCon North America 2026](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory/community/blob/main/articles/kubecon-2026-meet-the-experts.md) for the schedule.
- Browse the projects in [github.com/dsx-ai-factory](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory) and the community meetings cadence.

A follow-up article published after KubeCon will detail key takeaways from the workshop as well as outcomes from the initial community meetings.
