# Building DSX OS together: 30 days after our first contributor workshop

Our goal was to help partners become lasting contributors. Thirty days later, more than 60% of the workshop's external contributors had contributed again.

The people who deploy and operate AI factories bring experience that should help shape the software behind them. Our goal is to make contributing that experience to NVIDIA DSX OS a practical, ongoing collaboration.

That was the purpose of our September 30 contributor workshop. We brought partner engineers and NVIDIA maintainers together to work on real issues, review changes and build the relationships that make it easier to keep contributing.

Thirty days later, that collaboration was continuing. 20 of the 33 external contributors had contributed again, and 13 of the 21 pull requests opened during the workshop had been merged. These are early results, but they give us a concrete reason to keep investing in this format and to invite more companies to take part.
