# DSX AI Factory Communities

This page lists the open communities for DSX AI Factory projects, with the coordinates you need to take part: Slack channel, mailing list, meetings, meeting notes, and a public Drive folder for notes, recordings, and slides.

Everyone is welcome. You do not need to work at NVIDIA, be a customer, or have contributed before. Participation is governed by the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How to join

> **New to Slack here?** Follow the step-by-step guide: **[Join the DSX AI Factory Slack](join-slack.md)**.

1. Request an invite to the DSX AI Factory Slack workspace at [inviter.co/dsx-ai-factory](https://inviter.co/dsx-ai-factory), then join the community's channel.
2. Join the community's mailing list to receive meeting invites and announcements.
3. Follow the [DSX AI Factory Community calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=c_d83d62cba35a7b67c739c701dd77122feefff93389c4cc83b5c2b1d224c07c75%40group.calendar.google.com) for meetings across communities.

To learn how meetings run, see [community-meetings.md](community-meetings.md).

## How this page is maintained

[`communities.yaml`](communities.yaml) is the source of truth for every community's coordinates. This page shows the same information in a readable form.

A community can cover one project or several related projects. Each entry lists the repositories it covers.

To add or change a community:

1. Edit `communities.yaml`, using the fields described at the top of the file.
2. Update the table and the community's section on this page to match.
3. Open a pull request. A new community needs agreement from its product manager and technical lead before it is merged.
