# Join the DSX AI Factory Slack

DSX AI Factory communities talk in the **DSX AI Factory** Slack workspace. Anyone can join. You do not need to work at NVIDIA or be a customer.

## Step 1: Request an invite

1. Go to **[inviter.co/dsx-ai-factory](https://inviter.co/dsx-ai-factory)**.
2. Select **Slack**.
3. Submit the form.

## Step 2: Accept the invite

1. Check your email for the invitation to the DSX AI Factory workspace. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.
2. Open the invitation and create your Slack account, or sign in with an existing one.
3. Open the workspace at **[dsx-ai-factory.slack.com](https://dsx-ai-factory.slack.com)**.

## Step 3: Join your community's channel

Once you are in the workspace, open a link below, or select **Channels** > **Browse channels** in Slack and search for the channel name.

| Community | Channel |
| --- | --- |
| NVIDIA GPU Operator | `#gpu-operator` |
| NVIDIA DSX K8s Cluster Management | [#nvidia-dsx-k8s-cluster-management](https://dsx-ai-factory.slack.com/archives/C0CAR3S5HJ5) |
| NVIDIA DSX K8s Cluster Health & Configuration | [#nvidia-dsx-k8s-cluster-health-configuration](https://dsx-ai-factory.slack.com/archives/C0CFRCLLNS3) |
| NVIDIA Run:ai | `#run-ai` |

The [communities list](README.md) has each community's mailing list, meetings, and notes.

## Tips

- Introduce yourself in the channel: who you are and what you are working on.
- Ask questions in the channel rather than in direct messages, so others can find the answer later.
- Participation follows the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Need help?

If you can't join, [open an issue](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory/community/issues/new) in this repository and we'll help.
