## What shipped

One or two paragraphs. What is now available, in plain language, and where to get it
(repo link, release tag, package). If this is a deep-dive rather than a release post,
this section can be a single sentence pointing at the release post it follows.

## The problem it solves

Who had this problem, what they were doing before, why the old way was worse. Concrete,
not abstract — a scenario a reader recognizes.

## How it works

For a release post: the highlights, not the internals — what changed and why it
matters. For a deep-dive: architecture, key design decisions, the trade-offs that were
made and why, with enough detail that a reader could reason about whether it fits their
use case.

## What is next

What is planned, what is explicitly not planned, and where to follow along (issues,
discussions, roadmap doc) or get involved.
