Open with the point, not the throat-clearing. What is this post actually about, and why
does it matter to someone reading the DSX community repo right now.

## Context

What prompted this — a decision, an event, a pattern you noticed across components.
Enough background that a reader unfamiliar with the specifics can follow.

## The thing itself

The body of the post. For an exploratory post this is where the philosophy or the
governance question lives. For an event recap, this is what happened, what was
announced, and what to watch for.

## Why it matters, and what is next

Close by pointing outward — to a repo, a discussion, a follow-up post, or a decision
still open. Link out rather than restate.
